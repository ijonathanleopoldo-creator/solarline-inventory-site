Solarline Inventory Mobile Application

Solarline Inventory is the internal Android inventory and warehouse tracking system built for Solarline Power Solution. It empowers authorized warehouse administrators and staff to scan equipment barcodes, monitor real-time solar hardware stock levels, work offline seamlessly, and export Excel audit reports.

Read Privacy Policy Contact Administrator

Key Capabilities

Barcode & OCR Scanning

High-speed camera barcode and label text scanning for solar panels, inverters, batteries, and mounting hardware with instant stock lookup.

Offline-First Synchronization

Built on local SQLite with automatic cloud replication so warehouse teams can record stock-in and stock-out transactions even without internet access.

Role-Based Google SSO

Secure Google Single Sign-On restricted to authorized Solarline Power Solution personnel with separated Administrator and Staff permissions.

Excel Reports & Drive Backup

Generate formatted Excel (.xlsx) inventory and scan history reports locally or optionally back them up to a user-selected Google Drive folder.

Data Privacy & Google Account Usage

Solarline Inventory uses Google Sign-In strictly to verify authorized personnel email addresses and display names against our company access whitelist. Optional Google Drive permissions are requested only when an administrator explicitly exports an inventory report to Google Drive.

For complete details on how we handle, store, and protect user data, please review our Privacy Policy.
