Privacy Policy

Application: Solarline Inventory (com.solarlineinventoryapp)
Organization: Solarline Power Solution
Effective Date: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Solarline Power Solution (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when authorized personnel use the Solarline Inventory mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Access

Solarline Inventory is an internal enterprise warehouse management application. We collect only the minimum information required to operate the service:

  • Google Account Basic Profile (openid, email, profile): When you sign in with Google Single Sign-On (SSO), we receive your Google email address, display name, and account identifier. This is used strictly to verify whether your email address is authorized in our internal employee whitelist (Administrator or Staff role) and to attribute inventory scan logs.
  • Optional Google Drive Access (On-Demand Report Export Only): Google Drive permissions are not requested during sign-in. Only when an authorized Administrator explicitly uses the Google Drive Report Backup / Export feature does the app request permission to save generated Excel (.xlsx) inventory reports into a user-selected Google Drive folder.
  • Camera & Barcode Data (Local Processing): The app uses your device camera solely to scan product barcodes and read equipment serial/model labels via on-device ML Kit recognition. Camera frames are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or recorded as video/photos to external servers.
  • Inventory & Warehouse Activity Records: Product catalog items, stock counts, and scan timestamps created within the app are stored locally and synchronized across authorized company devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and enforce role-based access control (Administrator vs. Staff permissions).
  • To synchronize warehouse product quantities, stock movements, and scan history across authorized company devices.
  • To export inventory and audit spreadsheets to your device storage or, upon your explicit action, to your chosen Google Drive folder.
  • To deliver secure, authenticated in-app software updates to authorized company devices.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Limited Use Compliance: Solarline Inventory’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your Google account information or Google Drive data to any third party.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing profiles, or training artificial intelligence / machine learning models.
  • We do not read, modify, or delete personal Google Drive files unrelated to the Solarline Inventory reports you explicitly export.

4. Data Storage and Security

Inventory data and authentication sessions are stored locally on the mobile device using SQLite so the application can function reliably offline inside warehouse environments. When online, data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections to our cloud backend hosted on Supabase (PostgreSQL) and synchronized via PowerSync, protected by Row-Level Security (RLS) and cryptographic JSON Web Tokens (JWT).

5. Data Retention, Access Revocation, and Deletion

  • Revoking Google Permissions: You may sign out inside the app at any time or revoke Solarline Inventory’s access to your Google Account directly from your Google Account Permissions page.
  • Account & Data Removal: Administrators can remove an authorized user’s email from the application whitelist at any time under Settings → Authorized Profiles. To request complete deletion of your account record or associated user data, contact the administrator at the email below.
